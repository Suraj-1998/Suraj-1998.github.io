Featured project

ScoutMaster — a possession-adjusted scouting model

Ranking footballers on per-90 numbers turned out to rank them by how much possession their team had. A midfielder at a weak side spends twice as long defending, so she logs twice as many interceptions without being any better at it.

The fix rescales volume metrics to a common baseline. Its strength was calibrated by measuring the leftover correlation rather than adjusted until the output looked plausible — which turned out to need different exponents for defending and attacking.

Built as a Python pipeline feeding a star schema in Power BI, with user-weighted scoring written in DAX.
